Best AI Presentation Makers to Create Slides in Minutes

Published on October 24, 2026 • 5 min read

Creating compelling presentations used to be a time‑consuming chore, involving design know‑how, endless slide‑by‑slide tweaking, and countless hours of formatting. Today, AI‑powered presentation makers have turned the process on its head, letting you generate polished decks in minutes with just a few prompts.

In this review we evaluate four of the most popular AI presentation tools on the market. We’ll break down their core capabilities, pricing structures, and real‑world pros and cons, then give you a side‑by‑side comparison so you can pick the best fit for your workflow.

1. Beautiful.ai

Overview & Key Capabilities

  • AI‑driven design engine that automatically aligns, resizes, and styles slide elements.
  • Library of over 500 smart templates covering business, education, and marketing.
  • Real‑time collaboration with comments and version history.
  • One‑click brand kit integration for consistent colors, fonts, and logos.

Pricing

  • Free tier: Unlimited slides, access to basic templates, 5 MB file storage.
  • Pro: $12 / month (billed annually) – premium templates, brand kit, 100 MB storage.
  • Team: $24 / user / month – shared libraries, admin controls, priority support.

Pros

  • Fastest way to get a polished look without manual tweaking.
  • Strong brand management tools for teams.
  • Intuitive interface; low learning curve.

Cons

  • Limited animation options compared with traditional PowerPoint.
  • Free version caps file storage, which can be restrictive for heavy users.

2. Tome

Overview & Key Capabilities

  • Generative AI that creates narrative‑driven slide decks from a simple text prompt.
  • Built‑in storytelling modes (timeline, roadmap, pitch, report).
  • Supports multimedia – auto‑generated images, video embeds, and voice‑over narration.
  • Export to PDF, PPTX, or live web link.

Pricing

  • Free tier: Up to 5 decks, 10 slides per deck, basic AI generation.
  • Pro: $15 / month – unlimited decks, 100 slides per deck, custom branding.
  • Enterprise: Custom pricing – SSO, team admin, dedicated account manager.

Pros

  • Excellent for quickly turning a written outline into a full presentation.
  • Rich multimedia integration without extra tools.
  • Collaboration via shared live links.

Cons

  • AI sometimes produces generic visuals that need manual refinement.
  • Export to PowerPoint can lose some interactive elements.

3. Slidebean

Overview & Key Capabilities

  • AI layout engine that arranges content automatically based on hierarchy.
  • Extensive library of investor‑focused templates and pitch decks.
  • Analytics dashboard to track viewer engagement (views, time spent).
  • Integration with Google Slides and Dropbox for easy import/export.

Pricing

  • Free tier: 10 slides, basic templates, watermarked export.
  • Premium: $19 / month – unlimited slides, premium templates, no watermark.
  • Pro: $39 / month – advanced analytics, custom fonts, priority support.

Pros

  • Strong focus on startup and investor presentations.
  • Built‑in analytics help you measure deck performance.
  • Simple drag‑and‑drop after AI layout.

Cons

  • Less flexible for non‑business visual styles.
  • Higher price point for advanced analytics.

4. Gamma (by Microsoft)

Overview & Key Capabilities

  • AI‑assisted slide creation that suggests content, design, and data visualizations.
  • Seamless integration with Microsoft 365 (Word, Excel, Teams).
  • Live data linking – charts update automatically from source spreadsheets.
  • Supports AI‑generated speaker notes and summary slides.

Pricing

  • Free tier: Included with a Microsoft 365 Personal subscription – limited to 30 slides per month.
  • Business: $8 / user / month (Microsoft 365 Business Standard) – unlimited slides, premium AI features.
  • Enterprise: Part of Microsoft 365 E3/E5 bundles – advanced security, admin controls.

Pros

  • Deep ecosystem integration – perfect for organizations already on Microsoft 365.
  • Live data refresh saves time on reporting decks.
  • AI speaker notes improve presentation delivery.

Cons

  • AI suggestions can be overly conservative, requiring more manual tweaks.
  • Best features locked behind paid Microsoft 365 plans.

Final Verdict

If you need rapid, design‑first decks with consistent branding, Beautiful.ai offers the smoothest experience at a modest price. For storytelling‑centric presentations where you start from a plain text outline, Tome’s generative engine is unmatched. Slidebean shines for startup founders and anyone who wants built‑in performance analytics. Finally, organizations entrenched in Microsoft 365 will get the most value from Gamma’s live data integration and native collaboration features. Choose the tool that aligns with your primary workflow—design, narrative, analytics, or ecosystem—and you’ll be creating slide‑ready presentations in minutes rather than hours.

FAQs

Can I export AI‑generated slides to PowerPoint?

All four tools support export to PPTX, though some interactive elements (e.g., Gamma’s live data links) may revert to static images after export.

Do these tools offer real‑time collaboration?

Beautiful.ai, Tome, and Gamma provide live collaboration with comment threads. Slidebean offers shared links but its real‑time editing is less robust.
